# Indian E-Commerce Performance Analysis

End-to-end analytics on 1,00,000 orders (Jan 2023 – Dec 2025) using **Python, SQL and Power BI**.


## Dataset
9 related tables: orders, order_items, customers, products, shipments, payments, reviews, returns and marketing campaigns.

## What I Did
- **SQL:** Answered business questions on customers, products, revenue, returns and campaigns.
- **Python:** Cleaned the data (no duplicates found, missing values filled, dates fixed).
- **Power BI:** Built a 4-page dashboard: Executive Overview, Product & Sales, Customers, Operations.

## Key Insights
- Revenue ₹1.98bn, profit ₹158.6M, margin 8% (Delivered orders only).
- Electronics gives ~50% of revenue but only ~0.6% margin.
- Discounts above 20% make every order lose money.
- 58.9% of buyers have churned.
- 42.9% of deliveries are late, and late orders get lower ratings (3.37 vs 3.93).
